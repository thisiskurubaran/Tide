Tide is a code editor for iPhone and iPad made by Kurubaran Anandhan
("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use
Tide.

## The short version

Tide has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your projects stay on your
device unless you send them somewhere yourself.

## Your projects and files

Projects are stored in Tide's folder on your device. They are included in
your device backups (iCloud or computer) like other app data. We never
receive them.

Some features send data where you point them:

- **Git**: clone, pull and push talk to the Git host you use (for example
  GitHub), with the credentials you enter, which are kept in the device
  Keychain.
- **Packages**: installing npm or pip packages downloads them from the
  public npm registry and PyPI. Searches in the Packages screen are sent
  to those registries.
- **Remote mode**: your project and terminal input are sent to the Tide
  server whose address you enter. That server is run by you or your
  organisation, not by us.
- **Linked folders**: files are copied to and from the folder you choose,
  for example in iCloud Drive or another app.
- **Previews**: pages you preview load whatever they reference.

## Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive your
name, Apple Account or payment details; the App Store tells the app which
products your account owns.

## Crash reports

Crash reports are off unless you turn them on in Settings. When on, a
report of the crash (app version, device model, OS version and where the
code failed) is sent to Sentry, which processes it for us. Reports exclude
your code, file contents, project names and terminal output. You can turn
them off at any time.

## Contacting us

If you report a problem, the email you send, and the device details and
recent app logs attached to it (which you can read and edit before
sending), are used only to help you and fix the problem.

## Children

Tide is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal
information from them.
